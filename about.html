About Us

A local, owner-led company built on a simple promise: transparent, reliable, budget-friendly work — and one number to call for all of it.

Our Story

A decade of doing it right

Resistance Restoration & Remodeling LLC grew out of more than ten years of hands-on drywall and construction work across Southwest Florida.

Our founder, Jairo Teck, is a licensed Florida Gypsum Drywall contractor. That matters more than it sounds: drywall is the trade that decides whether a finished room looks seamless or looks repaired. It is also the trade most often rushed by crews who treat it as a step on the way to something else.

We built the company around doing that step properly — and then expanding outward into the work that surrounds it: restoration after storm and water damage, kitchen and bathroom remodels, and the everyday repairs that keep a Florida home standing up to the climate.

Today we serve Fort Myers, Sanibel Island, Pine Island and the surrounding communities. The motto has not changed since day one: one call solves it all.

What We Stand For

Our commitment to you

Three things we will not compromise on, on any job, at any size.

Transparency

Written, itemized quotes. No vague lump sums, no mid-project surprises, no pressure to add scope you did not ask for. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

Reliability

We show up when we say we will, and we finish when we said we would. If a material delay changes the schedule, you get a call — not silence.

Quality Workmanship
